# Acme Tools Returns Policy

## 1. General terms
Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery with proof of purchase.
Refunds go to the original payment method within 5 business days.
Items must be unused and in original packaging.

## 2. Garden equipment
This section covers lawn mowers, hedge trimmers and leaf blowers.
Returns are accepted within 14 days only.
A restocking fee of 15% applies to all opened items.
Fuel-powered items must be drained before return.

## 3. Cordless power tools
This section covers drills, impact drivers and circular saws.
Returns are accepted within 90 days if the tool is registered online.
No restocking fee applies.
Batteries are covered by a separate 2-year warranty.

## 4. Clearance items
Clearance items are final sale and cannot be returned.
